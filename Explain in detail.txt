1. Problem Statement

Medical consent forms are essential legal documents that explain medical procedures, their benefits, risks, and possible complications before treatment. However, these documents are often written using complex medical terminology and legal language, making them difficult for many patients to understand.

As a result, patients may sign consent forms without fully understanding the procedure, associated risks, or available alternatives. This reduces informed decision-making and can affect the quality of communication between healthcare providers and patients.

Our project addresses this problem by using Artificial Intelligence to convert complex medical consent forms into clear, patient-friendly summaries while ensuring that critical medical risks are not omitted.

2. Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

Extract text from medical consent forms in PDF or TXT format.
Simplify complex medical terminology into easy-to-understand language.
Preserve the original legal meaning of the consent document.
Automatically identify and extract medical risks and complications.
Verify whether important risks are retained in the simplified summary.
Compare multiple Large Language Models (LLMs) to determine which model provides the best balance of speed and completeness.
3. Methodology

Our solution consists of several stages.

Step 1 – Document Upload

The user uploads a medical consent form in PDF or TXT format through the web interface.

Step 2 – Text Extraction

The backend extracts readable text from the uploaded document using pdfplumber.

Step 3 – AI-Based Simplification

The extracted text is sent to a selected Large Language Model running locally through Ollama.

The AI generates a simplified summary divided into sections such as:

Procedure
Benefits
Risks
Recovery

making the document easier for patients to understand.

Step 4 – Risk Extraction

The system extracts all risks, complications, warnings, and side effects from the original consent form.

It also extracts risks mentioned in the generated summary.

Step 5 – Risk Verification

The extracted risks from both documents are compared.

If any important risk from the original document is missing in the simplified summary, it is highlighted.

Step 6 – Multi-Model Comparison

The same document is processed by multiple LLMs including:

Qwen 3.5
Llama 3.2
Gemma 3
Mistral
DeepSeek-R1

The system measures:

Processing time
Missing risks
Generated summary quality

allowing users to compare model performance.

The project is implemented using a client-server architecture.

Frontend
HTML
CSS
JavaScript

Provides:

File upload
Model selection
Multi-model comparison
Display of summaries and extracted risks
Backend

Developed using FastAPI.

Responsible for:

Receiving uploaded files
Extracting text
Communicating with Ollama
Running AI models
Risk verification
Returning results
AI Runtime

We use Ollama to run open-source Large Language Models locally.

Supported models include:

Qwen 3.5
Llama 3.2
Gemma 3
Mistral
DeepSeek-R1

Running models locally improves privacy because sensitive medical documents are not uploaded to external cloud services.

5. Results

The developed system successfully:

Generates patient-friendly summaries.
Preserves important medical information.
Automatically extracts medical risks.
Detects missing risks in simplified summaries.
Compares multiple AI models.
Measures processing time for each model.

Model	Response Time	Missing Risks
