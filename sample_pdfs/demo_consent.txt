MEDICAL SURGICAL CONSENT FORM

Procedure:
Appendectomy (Removal of Appendix)

Benefits:
Relief from appendicitis and prevention of serious infection.

Risks:
- Infection
- Bleeding
- Adverse reaction to anesthesia
- Blood clots
- Injury to nearby organs

Alternatives:
Antibiotic treatment may be considered in some cases.

Recovery:
Most patients recover within 2-4 weeks.

Consent:
I understand the procedure, risks, benefits, and alternatives.
